# Vantage-ARS business scope and competitive pricing

**Published service scope and price basis · Worldwide remote delivery · ZAR · researched 2 October 2026**

This document defines Vantage-ARS's practical VoIP and telecoms, networking, and IT infrastructure services for people and organisations operating real systems. Remote service is available worldwide; on-site work can be arranged worldwide subject to travel, local access, and project arrangements. Prices below are customer-facing rates in ZAR. Where VAT is chargeable, the written quote states the applicable VAT treatment; international customer tax treatment and currency conversion are confirmed on the written quote.

## 1. Business purpose and customer

Vantage-ARS helps individuals and organisations worldwide diagnose, plan, configure, improve, and support communications and IT infrastructure. The public site specifically identifies VoIP and telecoms, networking, and IT infrastructure, and confirms work scope, timing, and price before work is agreed.

**Best-fit customers:** small and medium businesses, offices, and operators that need practical help with business telephony, connectivity, local networks, or core IT infrastructure. This is a proposed target segment; confirm it against actual customers and capability.

**Positioning:** a clear, independent technical service with written scope, transparent labour and travel charges, and no work started until the customer approves the quote. Vantage-ARS should compete on clarity, diagnosis, and accountable delivery, not claim lowest price, 24/7 cover, certifications, or guaranteed response times without evidence.

## 3. Work included and excluded

### Included service work, when individually accepted in writing

- Requirements discovery and technical assessment for the agreed systems.
- VoIP, SIP, hosted PBX, telephony, and call-quality troubleshooting within access and supplier boundaries.
- Network setup, troubleshooting, configuration, and small defined changes.
- Practical IT infrastructure assessment, configuration, installation, and troubleshooting for systems within demonstrated competence.
- A written scope, assumptions, exclusions, customer responsibilities, price, schedule, acceptance checks, and change-control process for each job.
- A short completion note recording work performed, unresolved items, and customer handover information.

### Excluded unless specifically assessed, insured, qualified, and written into a separate quote

- 24/7 monitoring or response, guaranteed service levels, uptime guarantees, incident response, or security operations.
- Cybersecurity penetration testing, forensic work, regulated/compliance certification, electrical work, construction, and certified structured-cabling tests.
- Data recovery guarantees, unsupported vendor/platform changes, or work requiring credentials or access the customer cannot lawfully provide.
- Carrier/network-provider service charges, airtime/call charges, internet subscriptions, hardware, software licences, cloud subscriptions, taxes, permits, and third-party professional fees.
- Customer data migration, backups, or deletion unless explicitly specified with a verified recovery plan and customer authorisation.
- Unapproved emergency work, purchases, ongoing support obligations, or customer-facing claims about vendor partnership or certification.

## 4. End-to-end business operating scope

The business lifecycle is enquiry to approved work to documented close-out. The founder owns all commercial commitments and risk decisions.

1. **Marketing:** publish only owner-approved service descriptions and prices; capture enquiries; do not promise availability, outcomes, discounts, or delivery dates.
2. **Reception:** record the real customer's contact details, requested outcome, systems affected, urgency as stated, and preferred contact channel; do not diagnose beyond competence or promise a response time.
3. **Marketing review:** check fit and completeness; request missing details through an owner-reviewed message draft; make no offer or sales handoff without founder approval.
4. **Founder sales handoff:** review the enquiry, customer risk, fit, evidence, and next step; approve, decline, or request clarification with a recorded reason.
5. **Sales discovery and quote:** confirm stakeholders, access, environment, deliverables, acceptance criteria, dependencies, exclusions, VAT basis, travel, third-party costs, schedule, payment terms, and quote validity. Founder approves the final offer and any commitment.
6. **Approval and scheduling:** obtain explicit customer acceptance and any required deposits or supplier approvals under written terms. Do not incur costs or book non-refundable services before the founder's approval and customer authority are recorded.
7. **Technical delivery:** work only to the accepted scope; protect customer information; document configuration changes; stop and seek change approval if scope, cost, risk, or timing changes.
8. **Operations and close-out:** record work notes, tests, outstanding risks, customer handover, asset/vendor details supplied for the job, and acceptance. Do not claim resolved where not verified.
9. **Finance and accounting:** prepare quotes/invoice details and reconcile evidence under owner control. The current workspace does not post accounting entries, collect payments, or connect to accounting/payment services.
10. **Administration and governance:** manage access, records, backups, privacy requests, policies, and audit; limit access by role and retain records only under an approved retention policy.
11. **Customer support and risk:** log complaints, outages, safety/security concerns, disputes, and proposed customer outcomes; route material risk, refunds, credits, or commitments to the founder.

## 5. Commercial rules for every quote

- Quote in ZAR and state the applicable South African VAT treatment clearly. Where standard-rated VAT applies, show the 15% component. Confirm the customer's country and applicable tax treatment before invoicing cross-border work.
- Separate labour, travel, equipment, software, carrier/usage, taxes, and third-party items. Never bury recurring charges in a one-off fee.
- Define business hours and location before applying the rate card. Confirm appointment availability; the proposed after-hours rate is not an emergency SLA.
- Give each quote an expiry date, payment terms, scope, exclusions, assumptions, customer responsibilities, schedule, and acceptance method. Have a South African professional review customer terms and privacy processes before adoption.
- Require written customer approval before work; require founder approval for each offer, commitment, spend, change, refund, and material customer risk.
- For fixed-price work, estimate labour and supplier costs from an actual survey. Do not use the indicative ranges as guaranteed prices.
- Record the evidence supporting cost, price, and technical recommendation in the Vantage-ARS workspace.
